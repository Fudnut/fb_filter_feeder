# FluffBlock Purity: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

FluffBlock Purity ("the extension") is published by Steve Rose (beachie41@gmail.com). It filters selected content while you browse Facebook.

## Information processed

To filter, the extension examines content rendered on facebook.com pages inside your browser: visible page text, links, labels and page structure. This processing happens locally in your browser.

## Information sent to the developer

None. The extension does not transmit Facebook page content, posts, messages, names, account identifiers, cookies, authentication information or browsing history to the developer or anyone else. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking service.

## Settings

Ordinary on/off preferences use browser-provided synchronized storage, so your browser provider may sync them between your devices if you use browser sync. Custom word and phrase filters you type are stored locally in your browser and are not synced or transmitted by the extension.

## Permissions

- **Access to facebook.com and its subdomains:** lets the extension read the page so it can find and hide matching posts. It runs on Facebook only.
- **storage:** saves your filter settings.
- **activeTab and scripting:** let the toolbar popup start the extension's own packaged scripts in the current Facebook tab. No remote code is loaded.

## Diagnostics

"Export diagnostics" saves a file of detector information and counts to your device when you click it. "Export DOM" saves a troubleshooting file that can contain visible page text, author names and links. Neither file is uploaded automatically. Review the DOM file before you share it with anyone.

## Sale or sharing

The extension does not sell user information or share it with advertising networks, data brokers or other third parties, and does not use it for personalised, retargeted or interest-based advertising.

## Retention

The developer keeps no database of user browsing information. Stored preferences stay in your browser or browser sync service until you change, clear or remove them.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received through browser APIs by FluffBlock Purity adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only to provide the extension's user-facing filtering features.

## Third party platform

FluffBlock Purity is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Meta Platforms, Inc. Facebook is a trademark of Meta Platforms, Inc.

## Changes

If data handling changes, this policy and any required disclosures will be updated before the change takes effect.
